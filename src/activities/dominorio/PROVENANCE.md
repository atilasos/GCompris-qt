# Dominório

The QML, JavaScript and SVG in this activity are original implementations for
this GCompris fork, licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. No CRJM code, illustrations,
training text or assets have been copied.

The rules were checked against the CRJM platform by atilasos:
https://github.com/atilasos/crjm/tree/b206a7b4948673be935a5cb6a5a84df92d3ba6c2/src/games/dominorio

Reference behaviour: an empty 8×8 board, player 1 vertical and player 2
horizontal, alternating placement on two empty adjacent cells, and normal-play
victory when the next player has no legal placement. The native acceptance test
uses a move transcript generated by the reference implementation; it plays the
whole transcript through real controls and does not load or override game state.

The CRJM platform retains its original licence. This attribution grants no new
rights over that platform. No external distribution is performed by this work.

The local opponent, three difficulty policies, lesson, graded hints and decision
review are original teaching content. The opponent uses a bounded one-response
lookahead in a WorkerScript ES module and imports the same rules as the board.
No CRJM AI or training implementation is incorporated.
