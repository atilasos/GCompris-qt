# Gatos & Cães

The QML, JavaScript and SVG in this activity were written for this GCompris
fork and are licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. `gatos_caes.mjs` is an independent
implementation of the abstract rules, as its header states. No CRJM code,
illustrations, training text or assets have been copied.

Rules reference: CRJM platform by atilasos,
https://github.com/atilasos/crjm/tree/b206a7b4948673be935a5cb6a5a84df92d3ba6c2/src/games/gatos-caes

Reference behaviour: an empty 8×8 board, player 1 (Gatos) opening on one of the
four central cells, the first Cão outside those cells, no placement on an
occupied cell or orthogonally next to an animal of the other species, and
normal-play victory when the next player has no legal placement.

The CRJM platform retains its original licence. This attribution grants no new
rights over that platform and claims no GPL relicensing of it.

The local opponent, three difficulty policies, lesson, graded hints and decision
review are original teaching content for this fork. Acceptance evidence is
recorded under `.scratch/crjm-gcompris/issues/04-gatos-caes-duas-pessoas.md`
and `05-gatos-caes-tux-aprendizagem.md`.
