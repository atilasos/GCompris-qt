# Quelhas

The QML, JavaScript and SVG in this activity were written for this GCompris
fork and are licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. `quelhas.mjs` was authored from the
rules and is not a translation of CRJM code, as its header states. No CRJM
code, illustrations, training text or assets have been copied.

Rules reference: CRJM platform by atilasos,
https://github.com/atilasos/crjm/tree/b206a7b4948673be935a5cb6a5a84df92d3ba6c2/src/games/quelhas

Reference behaviour: an empty 10×10 board, player 1 vertical and player 2
horizontal, each move covering a segment of two or more consecutive empty
cells in the player's direction, no overlapping segments, and the orientation
swap offered to player 2 after the first move. The activity's own help text
states the end-of-game condition shown to players.

rights over that platform and claims no GPL relicensing of it.

The local opponent, three difficulty policies, lesson, graded hints and decision
review are original teaching content for this fork. Acceptance evidence is
recorded under `.scratch/crjm-gcompris/issues/08-quelhas-duas-pessoas.md` and
`09-quelhas-tux-aprendizagem.md`.
